What is LGD-Eval?

AI agents are taking over. We now install "skills" that run with our permissions, read our files, and talk to our APIs. But there is no standard way to evaluate them. No independent third party to audit them. No evidence that they are actually safe.

LGD-Eval is that independent third party. We scan, score, and gate any Claude/Cursor/Codex skill before you install it. We do not make the products we evaluate. We do not profit from the products we evaluate. We are independent.
